Zero Carry

Sixty-four pre-registered trials looking for a better signal returned nothing at all. The improvement, when it came, was one line in a broker's symbol table.

The book is a long-only trend follower on gold, run alongside a few uncorrelated sleeves — bitcoin, the Nasdaq, Brent. It has been at its frontier for about a year. Every attempt to improve the signal has failed, and this round was no different, so it is worth being precise about what failed before describing what worked.

Here is the line that mattered, read live from the terminal:

Symbol        swap_mode            spread   long %/yr
XAUUSD        POINTS         $0.42 (0.94bp)      -3.70
GoldEternal   DISABLED       $0.55 (1.23bp)       0.00

The same gold exposure, financed at nothing.

Five directions, all of them dead

Each round was pre-registered — parameters, assets and pass criterion fixed in writing before anything ran — and gated at matched volatility, because an overlay that only ever reduces exposure will beat a naive paired t-test by de-risking rather than by predicting.

  • Canonical factors — 0 of 7. Value as five-year reversal, betting-against-beta, cross-sectional skew, calendar seasonality. Never coded here before.
  • Volume — 0 of 17. Volume and dollar bars on an activity clock, volume-weighted price path, expansion gates, Amihud illiquidity.
  • Book-level risk overlays — 0 of 11. Volatility management, covariance-based targeting, drawdown sizing, a crisis-correlation gate.
  • Relative-value trend — 0 of 16. Fifteen same-session spreads: curve, crack, quality, tech-versus-broad.
  • Financing-aware weights — 0 of 13. Expected return set common across sleeves so only the known carry differed — no estimation error at all.

Two of those deserve a footnote. Long-horizon value came out at −0.62 Sharpe: on this universe, multi-year momentum persists rather than reverting. And the spread sleeves landed at a mean correlation of +0.03 to the book — exactly what the theory predicts, since the market factor cancels between the legs. The structure was right. There was simply no edge inside it to diversify into.

The largest number was never a signal

A year ago the book's financing cost was measured properly for the first time: −3.79% a year across the four sleeves, worth about −0.29 Sharpe. That made overnight funding the single biggest measured drag in the whole system — larger than spread, larger than slippage, and far larger than any signal effect ever found. The note written at the time said the only untested way to move it was execution, not research.

Then it was treated as a footnote for twelve months, subtracted at the end of each backtest and otherwise ignored. Nobody went and read the swap table.

Reading the swap table took an afternoon and beat a year of signal research.

What the census found

Querying every symbol on the venue for swap_mode, contract size and live spread turns up five instruments that charge no overnight financing at all. One of them is gold.

Six of sixty-eight. There is no swap-free index, crypto or energy instrument on the venue — which is precisely why the gain below is confined to the gold sleeve.

The objection, and the test

A perpetual instrument that charges no swap has to earn its funding somewhere, and the obvious place is the price. So the log basis of GoldEternal against XAUUSD was regressed on time: the drift is +0.17% a year across 2,711 hourly bars, inside a band about 1% wide, at a daily-return correlation of 0.998. Nothing resembling the −3.70% it avoids. The instrument has only 167 days of history, which excludes a large embedded drift but not a small one — so it gets re-measured monthly rather than believed.

The same question applied in reverse to the positive-carry instruments, and natural gas settled it. NGAS charges −165.9% a year while its price has drifted −0.99% a year over fifteen years. Prices on this venue are spot-like and swap carries the entire roll. The swaps are additive, not double-counted.

Which raises a tempting number: sugar pays +21.6% a year to hold long, cocoa +14.8%. A long-only trend follower on sugar would collect that on top of any trend edge. But those are one snapshot of a rate that moves enormously through time, and there is no historical commodity term structure to test them against. Applying today's sugar carry across an eighteen-year backtest would manufacture a 22%-a-year sleeve out of a single afternoon's term structure. So there is no number for commodity carry here. It stays open.

What actually changed

All figures net of real broker carry and spread, compounded onto a common business-day index. That last detail matters more than it sounds: bitcoin quotes at weekends, so the book's series ran at roughly 365 days a year while the gold sleeve ran at 259, and annualising both with the same √252 inverted the ranking. The first version of this comparison was wrong for exactly that reason.

Net Sharpe improves by 22%, and it splits cleanly in two: +0.100 from moving gold onto the swap-free instrument, and +0.116 from overweighting gold now that its net edge is visibly larger than the others'.

Three things this is not

The tilt is not established. Its paired t-statistic at matched volatility is +0.75 against equal weight, on five of nine positive years, against a threshold of +1.50 used everywhere else in this work. What supports it is that the driver is contractual rather than estimated, and that the response is a plateau — 40% gold gives +1.200, 50% gives +1.212, 60% gives +1.196 — rather than a spike. That is suggestive. It is not proof, and calling it proof would be the easiest mistake available.

Leverage is not edge. Removing an external drawdown limit takes this book from about 8.5% a year to roughly 50%, at a −46% maximum drawdown. Sharpe does not move an inch, because Sharpe is invariant to leverage. That is a decision about tolerance dressed up as a discovery, and the distinction is worth defending.

The signal did not improve. Not once, across sixty-four honest attempts. Everything above came from what the position costs to hold, not from knowing where gold is going.

Measure what you pay before optimising what you predict

Costs are contractual. They are published, they are deterministic, and they need no out-of-sample validation — a saving of 3.70% a year is 3.70% a year whether the strategy works or not. Signals are estimated, noisy, and mostly imaginary. A research programme that spends every hour on the second category while carrying the first as a footnote is optimising the harder half of the problem.

Carry is also venue-specific, which is its own warning. Brent earns +9.6% a year at one broker and pays −4.81% long at another. A book tuned on one venue's financing is mis-specified the moment it moves.

Backtests run 2018–2026 on H4 gold and daily sleeves, costs in basis points from live spreads widened 1.5×, drawdowns close-to-close and therefore optimistic against real intraday excursions. The swap-free instrument is unverified for fill quality and its eligibility under the venue's own rules; neither is assumed here.
